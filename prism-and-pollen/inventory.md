## The writing you actually have

### 1. Cardinal — *The Stanford Corridor*
**Repo:** [github.com/kylesmcauliffe/cardinal](https://github.com/kylesmcauliffe/cardinal)  
**Download:** [Cardinal.pdf](https://github.com/kylesmcauliffe/cardinal/blob/main/Cardinal.pdf)

Narrative nonfiction. Not a memoir. Four acts. Argument: a forty-mile Peninsula corridor has been the controlling position in the American economy for 140+ years; Stanford is the secret heart of California; the garage story is a theodicy.

**Mechanism (three parts, every era):**
1. Chokepoint control
2. Public subsidy converted into private monopoly
3. Elite network reproduction

**Four questions every chapter answers:** Who controls the inputs? Who captures the surplus? Who is excluded? How does the system reproduce?

**Voice:** Michael Lewis meets *Guns, Germs, and Steel*. Confident, not arrogant. Specificity as drama. Not academic, not hagiographic, not conspiratorial, not breathless. Gravity is not a conspiracy.

**Opens:** Galvez Street (the administrator who ordered the Alta California missions).  
**Ends:** 2025 inauguration platform — tech founders closer to the president than most of the cabinet.  
**Method:** museum / object first (street sign, land grant, newspaper, criminal record).

**Acts (current numbering):**
- **I — Land, Monopoly, and Institution** (missions → railroad → Jane Stanford’s will)
- **II — Hoover, War, and the Archive State**
- **III — The Silicon Valley Machine**
- **IV — The Open Era** (platforms, Palantir, AI, moral ledger, street names, traveler’s tour)

Twilda still labels this *“A Subliminal History of Silicon Valley.”* That subtitle is the older name.

### 2. Dawn — *An American Odyssey*
**Download:** [Dawn.pdf](https://github.com/kylesmcauliffe/cardinal/blob/main/Dawn.pdf)

Companion volume. Three thousand years of empire, collapse, and reinvention in the old world, to the eve of the American conquest of California. Read Dawn first for origins; Cardinal stands alone for the argument.
